Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book an event? +

We recommend booking at least 3–4 weeks in advance for large setups. However, for small gatherings, college events, and intimate celebrations, we offer flexible packages that can be set up in just 5–7 days. Just reach out and we'll work around your timeline!

Which areas do you serve? +

GiftAura is based in Navi Mumbai and proudly serves Vashi, Nerul, Belapur, Kharghar, Koparkhairane, Ghansoli, and surrounding areas. We also deliver to local colleges and hostels in these zones!

What is your cancellation and refund policy? +

Because every event and decoration is custom‑planned, we require a 50% advance payment to confirm your booking. Cancellations made 7+ days before the event receive a full refund. For small gatherings, we offer flexible rescheduling at no extra cost.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, credit/debit cards, and bank transfers. For college students, we also accept split payments so you and your friends can contribute easily!

How do I stay updated about my event planning? +

We add you to a dedicated WhatsApp group with regular updates – from design mockups to setup timelines. For small parties, we share quick video previews so you know exactly how your space will look before we arrive.

Do you handle small gatherings, college events, or hostel parties? +

Absolutely! While other companies only focus on big weddings, we believe every celebration matters – big or small. Whether it's a college farewell, a hostel room surprise, a study group victory party, or a cozy dinner with 5–10 friends – we customize the decor, themes, and budget just for you. You don't need a huge event to get our personalized touch!
