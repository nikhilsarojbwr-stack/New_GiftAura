Everything you need to know before booking with GiftAura.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does customization take? +

We recommend at least 2 weeks in advance for fully custom decorations and gifts. However, we can accommodate last‑minute requests depending on the complexity – just reach out and we’ll try our best!

Do you ship internationally? +

Currently, GiftAura operates in Navi Mumbai and surrounding areas. For deliveries outside India, we can arrange shipping on a case‑by‑case basis – please contact us for a custom quote.

Can I return a custom gift? +

Because every gift is made to order, we don't offer returns on custom items. However, if there's an issue with quality or craftsmanship, we’ll work with you to make it right – your satisfaction is our priority.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, credit/debit cards, and bank transfers. We’ll send you a secure payment link after confirming your order.

How do I track my order? +

Once your order is confirmed, we’ll share regular updates via WhatsApp or email. For deliveries, we’ll provide tracking details and a delivery window so you know exactly when to expect us.

Do you offer corporate gifting? +

Absolutely! We specialise in corporate gifting – from custom hampers to branded gifts for employees, clients, and events. Reach out to us and we’ll design a package that fits your needs and budget.
